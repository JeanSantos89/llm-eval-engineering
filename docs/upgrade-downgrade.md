# Changing Plans (Upgrade & Downgrade)

You can change plans mid-cycle from **Settings → Billing → Change plan**. Upgrades and
downgrades behave differently.

## Upgrades — immediate

An upgrade takes effect **immediately**. You are charged a prorated amount for the
remainder of the current billing period at the difference between the two plan prices.
All higher limits and features unlock right away.

## Downgrades — at the end of the cycle

A downgrade is **scheduled for the end of the current billing period**. You keep the
higher plan's limits and features until then, and no refund is issued for the
difference (the prorated leftover is not returned; see the refund policy for what is and
is not refundable).

## Downgrades and usage limits

A downgrade can put you over the new plan's limits. If, at the moment the downgrade
takes effect, your workspace exceeds the lower plan's limits (for example more tracked
subscriptions than the new plan allows), the workspace is placed in the same
**over-limit state** described in the usage-limits policy: existing data stays readable,
but you cannot add new items until you are back under the limit. The downgrade is not
blocked or reversed — you are simply expected to reduce usage.

## Annual plans

Downgrading from an annual plan follows the same end-of-cycle rule, but the "end of
cycle" is the annual anniversary date, which may be far in the future.
