# PlanFlow Plans

PlanFlow offers three subscription plans. All prices are in USD and billed per workspace.

## Basic — $19/month

- Up to 3 team members
- Up to 500 tracked subscriptions
- Email support (response within 2 business days)
- Monthly billing only
- 1 GB of attachment storage

## Pro — $49/month

- Up to 15 team members
- Up to 5,000 tracked subscriptions
- Priority support (response within 8 business hours)
- Monthly or annual billing (annual saves 15%)
- 20 GB of attachment storage
- Usage analytics dashboard

## Enterprise — $149/month

- Unlimited team members
- Unlimited tracked subscriptions
- Priority support with a dedicated SLA (response within 1 business hour, 99.9% uptime commitment)
- Monthly or annual billing (annual saves 20%)
- 500 GB of attachment storage
- Usage analytics dashboard
- SSO / SAML login
- Audit log export

## A note on "priority support"

Both Pro and Enterprise include "priority support", but they are not the same tier.
Pro priority support means an 8-business-hour response target with no uptime guarantee.
Enterprise priority support adds a contractual SLA: a 1-business-hour response target
and a 99.9% uptime commitment. If a document or agent refers to "priority support"
without naming the plan, the guarantees differ and the plan must be specified.
