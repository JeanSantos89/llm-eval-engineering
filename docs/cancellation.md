# Cancellation

You can cancel a PlanFlow subscription at any time from **Settings → Billing → Cancel plan**.

## When cancellation takes effect

Cancellation is not immediate. Your plan stays active until the end of the current
billing period, and you keep full access until that date. You are not billed again
after you cancel.

- Monthly plans: access ends on the next billing date.
- Annual plans: access ends on the anniversary date of the subscription.

## Grace period for data

After the plan ends, your workspace enters a **30-day grace period**. During the grace
period the workspace is read-only: you can export data but cannot add or edit
subscriptions. After 30 days the workspace and all its data are permanently deleted and
cannot be recovered.

## Reactivation

If you resubscribe during the grace period, the workspace returns to full access with
all data intact. Resubscribing after the grace period starts a new, empty workspace.

## Refunds on cancellation

Cancelling does not automatically issue a refund. Whether you receive any money back,
and how much, depends on the refund policy and how long ago you were charged. See the
refund policy for the exact conditions.
