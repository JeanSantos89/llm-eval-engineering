# Refunds

Refunds are always calculated from the **most recent charge**, not from the original
signup date.

## Full refund — first 7 days only

You are eligible for a **full refund** of the most recent charge if you request it
within **7 calendar days** of that charge. This applies to both monthly and annual
plans. A full refund closes the subscription immediately.

## Partial refund — after 7 days

After the 7-day window, refunds become **partial and prorated**. We refund the unused
portion of the current billing period, counted in whole unused days, minus a
non-refundable service fee of 10% of the amount charged.

Example: on an annual plan charged $499, cancelled with 200 unused days out of 365,
the prorated amount is refunded minus the 10% fee.

## When no refund applies

- Monthly plans cancelled after the 7-day window with fewer than 5 unused days in the
  period receive no refund (the prorated amount rounds to zero).
- Charges older than 12 months are never refundable.

## How refunds relate to cancellation

Requesting a refund is a separate action from cancelling. A full refund cancels the
subscription for you. A partial refund does **not** cancel the plan by itself — you must
also cancel if you want the subscription to end. This means it is possible to receive a
partial refund and still have an active subscription until you cancel.
