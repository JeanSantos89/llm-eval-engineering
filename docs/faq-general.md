# General FAQ

Short answers to common questions. For full rules, follow the linked policies.

**Can I try PlanFlow for free?**
There is a 14-day free trial on the Pro plan. No credit card is required to start it.
The trial does not apply to Basic or Enterprise.

**Which plan includes SSO?**
SSO / SAML is Enterprise only. See the plans document.

**How fast is support?**
It depends on your plan. Response targets differ across Basic, Pro, and Enterprise —
and "priority support" on Pro is not the same guarantee as on Enterprise. See the plans
document for the exact targets.

**If I cancel, do I get my money back?**
Cancelling and refunding are two different actions. Whether you get money back depends
on the refund policy (the 7-day full-refund window, then prorated). Cancelling alone
does not trigger a refund.

**What happens to my data if I stop paying?**
Your workspace becomes read-only for a 30-day grace period, then is permanently deleted.
See the cancellation policy.

**Do I get charged extra if I go over my limits?**
No. PlanFlow never charges overage. You enter an over-limit state where you cannot add
new items until you reduce usage or upgrade. See the usage-limits policy.

**Can I downgrade right now to pay less this month?**
Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period, not immediately, and
the unused difference is not refunded. See the upgrade-downgrade policy.

**Does PlanFlow integrate with SAP / Salesforce / QuickBooks?**
This FAQ does not cover third-party integrations. If it is not documented, treat it as
unsupported rather than assuming it exists.
