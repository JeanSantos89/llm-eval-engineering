# Usage Limits

Each plan has limits on team members, tracked subscriptions, and attachment storage.
See the plans document for the exact numbers per plan.

## What happens when you hit a limit

PlanFlow never charges overage fees and never silently drops data. Instead, the
behavior depends on which limit you hit.

### Tracked subscriptions and team members

When you reach the limit, the workspace enters an **over-limit state**:

- Existing data stays fully readable and editable.
- You cannot **add** new subscriptions or invite new members until you are back under
  the limit (by removing items or upgrading).
- No extra charge is applied and nothing is deleted.

### Attachment storage

When you reach your storage limit:

- You cannot upload new attachments.
- Existing attachments are unaffected.
- Uploading is blocked, not throttled — there is no slow-mode; it is a hard stop until
  you free space or upgrade.

## Important: no automatic upgrades

Hitting a limit never upgrades you automatically and never bills you extra. The only way
to raise a limit is to change plans yourself. This is different from services that
auto-charge for overage.

## Limits after a downgrade

A downgrade can place you over the new plan's limits. That case is handled the same way
as any over-limit state, and is described in the upgrade-downgrade policy.
